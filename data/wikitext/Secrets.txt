
==Dúnedain tracks and caches (Eradan)==
The caches and "secret areas" are often found by following rangers tracks. The tracks are recognized as blue glowing footsteps on the ground. You will recognize a cache by the glowing blue star symbol placed on the wall.

==About different lootables==


<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">The different lootables have higher/lower chance of rare items.</span></p>

<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">Breakables (Boxes, barrels, urns etc)</span></p>
*<span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">Usually containing silver, arrows/bolts and sometimes health/power potions and in a few cases items of varying quality.</span>
*<span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">Often specific quests require you to break boxes and barrels for the quest-specific loot. Specifically Work of Westernesse in Fornost and the Barrow Downs, Elfstones in Ettenmoors and A No Gem No Mine Can Yield in Mirkwood.</span>
<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">Pile of Junk</span></p>
*<span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">In the junkpiles there are often not any uncommon, rare items. They more rather have common, poor quality junk items and often silver. The chance to get uncommon or rare items is small but it does happen.</span>
<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">Wooden chests</span></p>
*<span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">Wooden chests have a smaller chance of uncommon/rare items compared to gold ornament chests. Whenever the wooden chests do drop uncommon/rare items It's usually less than what can be found in their golden counterparts. This doesn't mean wooden chests are bad in comparison of gold ornament chests, in wooden chests you can find an elf-stone once in a while.</span>
<p class="MsoNormal"><span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">Gold ornament chests</span></p>
*<span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">Has a higher chance of containing more uncommon/rare loot and usually drops a larger amount of those items compared to wooden chests.</span>
*<span lang="EN-US" style="mso-ansi-language:EN-US">Often has quest items related. Specifically Seers words at Fornost, Cult of the Lidless Eye Scrolls which are spread over the many levels and the Mithril for the Truesilver quest at Mt. Gundabad.</span>
