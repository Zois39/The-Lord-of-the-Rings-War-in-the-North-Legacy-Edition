Below is a list of all the best equipment found in LOTR War in the North. Most of the best items in the game are found on Legendary mode but not all of them. Gear with additional elf-stone slots are often the best given they can add major bonus. For ever type of equipment in the game there is a two slot item, three slot items do exist but the only confirmed one is on normal mode at the Nordinbad NPC shop after the siege. The weapon is a Champion (Dwarf) Only war axe named Buri's Battle-axe. 

==Amulets - Amuletos==
[[File:Northwarden´s Amulet.png|thumb]]

=== Northwarden's Amulet - Amuleto de Mayoral del Norte ===

* Value: 21,860 / Valor: 21,860.
* +8 Strength / +8 Fuerza
* +135 Damage / +135 Daño
* Elfstone Slots: +2 / Ranuras de piedra élfica: +2
* Mithrill Upgrades: (6) w/2 slot covered, (7) w /1 slot covered and (8) with 2 free slots.

Loot obtained from a guild chest. Specifically, it is found on the Mount Gundabad map—past the second group of towers and before the save point visible at the tunnel entrance. The chest is located by following a Ranger secret on the left-hand side, just before the checkpoint. (The drop rate is moderate; you simply need to farm that spot until you get it.). The two elf-stone slots, in addition to existing bonuses, make it the best amulet in the game. (updated to legacy version 2026). only in Legendary mode. 
[[File:Dwarfmade trincket.png|thumb]]

=== Dwarfmade trinket - Amuleto Estrella de Sol ===

* Value: 380 / Valor: 380
* Elfstone Slots: +2 / Ranuras de piedra élfica: +2
* Mithrill Upgrades: (6) w/2 slot covered, (7) w /1 slot covered and (8) with 2 free slots.

Can be bought from Bree NPC only on Normal and Heroic mode. (Just a tip: don't save it until the Legendary level; use it in the campaign with two Brilliant Champion Stones, or the best stones you can get, because the Northwardens Amulet surpasses it in every way, in later game).

=== Seer´s Talisman - Amuleto Cabeza de León ===
