Glorhirin is an elf, who lives in Mirkwood. Whe serves Thranduil's Warden, with his friend, [[Galron]].

Glorhirin is a NPC character, which can be found in "Captive Side Quest". If player follows tracks of uruks in [[Mirkwood]], after killing a horde of group, they found Glorhirin. He says he is a warden of Thranduil, who later being ambushed by spiders and orcs front of Radagast's House. He says he is taken a prisoner, but he don't knows fate of Galron. Later then a long talk, Glorhirin leaves group for finding his friend.

At the end of Mirkwood, Glorhirin comes and says his friend was killed in this fight. He later gives Eredan / Farin / Andriel[[Eradan| ]]Galron's amor. Player can accept or decline this offer.

He is later fate is unknown.
