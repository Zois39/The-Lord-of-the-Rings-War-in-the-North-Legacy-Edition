[[File:Nordinbad.jpg|thumb|400px|The Dwarven fortress of Nordinbad.]]
Well hidden within the Grey Mountains, Nordinbad is a refuge for a small band of Dwarves of Durin’s Folk who remained there when the rest of their kin fled those mountains to escape the attacks of dragons long ages ago. Their cavern home is built around an underground lake, Azan-zâram. These secretive folk are ruled by Gorin of the Longbeards. He and his people are strong allies in the fight against Agandaûr, yet their valor threatens to expose this fortress to the unyielding forces of darkness.
