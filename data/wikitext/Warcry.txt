War-cry is a first-tier active skill for [http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Farin Farin] in the left set. Like all first-tier skills it is set in the middle column, is the only option within that tier, and has one rank.

In-game description: "Press RT+B to temporarily increase Armor, Health, and melee damage."

War-cry can be enhanced by the following skills:

*[http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Dwarf_Armor Dwarf Armor], [http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Taunt Taunt], and [http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Retaliation Retaliation] in the left set.

*[http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Fortitude Fortitude], [http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Abundant_Harm Abundant Harm], and [http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Indomitable_Spirit Indomitable Spirit] in the center set.
*[http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Battle_Fury Battle Fury] and [http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Battle-ready Battle-ready] in the right set.
Armor is increased by 100% of the base armor

Health is increased by 25% of the base health
