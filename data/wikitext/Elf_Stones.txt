
==How to use==
*PC: Double-click the Elf-stone and use it on the equipment piece of choice.

*PS3: Select the Elf-stone and press X. All equipment slots in your bags will highlight if they contain items that can hold the Elf-stone. Select the equipment slot, and then highlight the individual piece of equipment in your inventory. Press X when you are ready to insert the Elf-stone into an open socket. Note: once filled, both the socket and the Elf-stone cannot be used again. Slotting Elf-stones is permanent.

*360: ~

''Note:'' some Elf-stones can only be used on Melee weapons. Others can be used on ''any'' weapon, including rangeed weapons like bows and crossbows (staves are considered Melee weapons). Some Elf-stones can be used on both armor and weapons (melee, ranged, shields and armor).

==Grades==
There are five grades of Elf-stones:
*Normal
*Glowing
*Shining
*Radiant
*Legendary
