

<p style="color:rgb(1000,1000,1000);">Mirrors are located at the settlements in Lord of the Rings War in the North, and they allow the player to customize the characters appearence.</p>

<p style="color:rgb(1000,1000,1000);">When the Mirror is activated the player can choose from a list of customizations for:</p>

*Face
*Hair
*Eyes
*Beard

==Locations==

*Bree - At the start of the game (when done talking to Aragorn) if you go to the right of Aragorn and head towards a fireplace in the right-hand corner of the tavern.
*Sarn Ford - At the back of the large cabin.
