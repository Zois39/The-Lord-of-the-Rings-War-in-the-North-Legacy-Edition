Active Skill

Cost: 19 power

'''LT + RB''' fires a precise high damage bolt at your target.

Note: Additional ranks increase bolt damage.

The damage done by this skill is the total ranged damage plus 100% of the total ranged damage.

Rank 1 - +100% of total ranged damage

Rank 2 - +120% of total ranged damage

Rank 3 - +140% of total ranged damage

Equation: Total ranged damage (Min. or Max) + Rank (1, 2, or 3) = Total Damage for Heavy Bolt
