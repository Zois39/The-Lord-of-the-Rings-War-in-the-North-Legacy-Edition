Active Skill

Cost: 15 power 

Press '''RT + B''' to temporarily increase Armor, Health, and melee damage.

Note: Can be enhanced by the following skills: [[Dwarf Armor]], [[Taunt]], [[Retaliation]], [[Fortitude]], [[Abundant_Harm|Abundant Harm]], [[Indomitable Spirit]], [[Battle Fury]], and [[Battle-ready]]

Armor of equipment is increased by 100% of the base armor.

Health is increased by 25% of the base health
