In the list below you'll find some numbers (2-6) with the added effect. If you wear 2 items from one armor set, you get the bonus next to "2:". If you wear 3 items, you get the bonus from "2:" and "3:", etc. So armor sets can be a great way to make your character even more powerful!

Notes: Some sets are character specific. Some sets were included downloads with pre-ordered games.
