==The Quest for Mithril==
After chapter 2, Barrow-Downs, you're located in the elven city Rivendell. From there, talk to Arwen on the water bridge to get a quest called "Miruvor" that asks you to collect herbs in the Ettenmoors (chapter 3). When you have collected them, hand the quest in to Arwen and you'll recieve a follow-up quest called "Truesilver". This will ask you to find Mithril ore. Enter Mount Gundabad (chapter 4) with a dwarf in the party that is not AI. Walk up the mountain. When you get to the first troll that walks out of a cave, go inside that cave and Farin should be able to break down a wall and find a chest with the Mithril inside. After finishing the chapter, hand it in to Arwen and then talk to the Rivendell blacksmith called Angmir to unlock a Rune upgrade bonus for one piece of equipment.
