Passive Skill

During [http://witn.wikia.com/wiki/Warcry War-cry], melee damage absorbed by the Champion is reflected back to the enemy.

Note: [[War-cry]] must be active for effect. Reflected damage is a percentage of your total Armor. Each rank adds damage.

The percentage is 12% of your total armor

Rank 1 - 12% of total armor

Rank 2 - 24% of total armor

Rank 3 - 36% of total armor

Equation: Total armor x Rank (1, 2, or 3) = Total damage
