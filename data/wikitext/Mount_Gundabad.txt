The great northern wastes of Middle-earth are separated from the lands of Eriador and Rhovanion by the formidable peaks of the Misty Mountains and the Grey Mountains. At the point where these two ranges meet there stands the imposing spire of Mount Gundabad. The treacherous path across the mountain is marked by dangers such as snow, avalanches, and freezing cold temperatures. Yet even travelers capable of withstanding these natural forces must then face the various defenses established by the Orcs.

The depths of Gundabad have fallen to the Orcs over time and have become one of their most powerful strongholds. They will go to any length to dissuade any who are either bold or desperate enough to challenge the mountain and its current inhabitants.
