==Class Specific Weapons==



<p style="color:rgb(1000,1000,1000);">Champion (Farin/Dwarf) ---> Crossbows, 2-Handed Swords, 2-Handed Axes, 2-Handed Blunts</p>

<p style="color:rgb(1000,1000,1000);">Ranger (Eredan/Man) ---> Bows, 1-Handed Weapons (can duel-wield with Dual Strike Skill), 2-Handed Swords</p>

<p style="color:rgb(1000,1000,1000);">Lore-Master (Andriel/Elf) ---> Staves, 1-Handed Weapons (with Skill)</p>

Listed Value may be from player Inventory or Store.
