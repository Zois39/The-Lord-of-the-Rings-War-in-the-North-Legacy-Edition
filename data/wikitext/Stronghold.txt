The Stronghold armor set.

Usable by all characters. This [[Armor sets|Armor Set's]] Origin is Man.


=='''Stacking Effects'''==

Armor Set effects are additive. In this set wearing five (5) pieces adds +50 <u>and</u> +110 Armor for a total bonus of +160 Armor.

2: +3 Stamina

3: +50 Armor

4: +5 Reduced Damage

5: +110 Armor

6: +65 Health
