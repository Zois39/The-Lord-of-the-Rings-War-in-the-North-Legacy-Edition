'''Orcs''' were a breed of creatures that dwell in Isengard. When Melkor was taken in chains to Valinor, the Orcs were forced to flee from Angband. They are similar to other orcs in appearance, but are slightly smaller, sometimes hunched over or appearing to walk and run with limps.
